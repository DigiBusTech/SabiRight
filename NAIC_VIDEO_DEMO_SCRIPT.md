# NAIC 3-5 Minute Video Demonstration Script
**National AI Innovation Challenge — Technical Demonstration Guide**
*Target Length: 3 minutes 30 seconds to 4 minutes 30 seconds*

---

### Segment 1: Introduction & Problem Context (0:00 - 0:45)
* **Visual:** Speaker on camera with title card: *"SabiRight — Sovereign Multilingual AI Legal First-Aid on N-ATLAS"*. Screen transitions to the SabiRight Homepage (`/`).
* **Audio / Script:**
  > *"Greetings, judges and evaluators of the National AI Innovation Challenge. I am presenting **SabiRight**, Nigeria's first sovereign, voice-first civic and legal intelligence platform built directly on **N-ATLAS** (`NCAIR1/N-ATLaS`).
  > 
  > In Nigeria today, over 70% of citizens cannot afford or access legal advice when confronted with arbitrary police stops, unlawful evictions, or workplace exploitation. Even when laws exist to protect them, those laws are trapped in dense English legalese. 
  > 
  > SabiRight breaks this barrier by pairing Nigeria's sovereign language model, N-ATLAS, with everyday channels: Web, Mobile, WhatsApp, and Telegram, allowing any Nigerian to speak or type in their local language and receive immediate, statutory protection."*

---

### Segment 2: Multilingual Voice & Chat in Action (0:45 - 2:00)
* **Visual:** Screen recording of the SabiRight Web / Mobile Interface (`/app/civic`).
* **Action:**
  1. Click the microphone icon or type a query in **Nigerian Pidgin**.
  2. Speak / Enter: *"Officer stop me for road say make I open my phone make e search my WhatsApp chat. Wetin the law talk about this?"*
* **Audio / Script:**
  > *"Let's test this in real-time. Here, a citizen asks in Nigerian Pidgin about their rights when stopped by law enforcement. 
  > 
  > When the audio or message is sent, SabiRight routes the speech through the N-ATLAS ASR and LLM pipeline, grounded by our Administrative Statutory MOAT.
  > 
  > Look at N-ATLAS's response: It communicates in natural, de-escalating Nigerian Pidgin without sounding robotic. Crucially, it cites **Section 37 of the 1999 Constitution (Right to Privacy)** and **Section 49 of the Police Act 2020**, providing step-by-step de-escalation instructions, and offering to match the citizen with a verified human lawyer if needed."*

---

### Segment 3: Omnichannel Experience (WhatsApp & Telegram) (2:00 - 2:45)
* **Visual:** Mobile screen recording showing a WhatsApp and Telegram conversation with the SabiRight Bot.
* **Action:**
  1. Send a voice note in Yoruba or Hausa: *"E kaasan, se landlord le le mi jade laarin ose meji?"* (Good afternoon, can a landlord evict me within two weeks?)
  2. Bot immediately replies in Yoruba citing the Tenancy Law.
* **Audio / Script:**
  > *"To ensure true accessibility, SabiRight is omnichannel. The citizen does not even need to install a new app. Through our official WhatsApp Cloud API and Telegram bot integrations, a market trader or artisan can send a voice note in Yoruba, Hausa, or Igbo. 
  > 
  > N-ATLAS transcribes the dialect, matches the relevant tenancy statutes, and answers back in their native vernacular in seconds."*

---

### Segment 4: Dual-Mode Sovereign Architecture & Admin Control (2:45 - 3:30)
* **Visual:** Transition to the SabiRight Super Admin Dashboard (`/admin`), navigating to the **API Keys & AI** tab.
* **Action:**
  1. Highlight the green **"🇳🇬 National AI Challenge Mode: Sovereign N-ATLAS"** banner.
  2. Click **"Test N-ATLAS Output"** button. The terminal card shows live diagnostic feedback in Pidgin from `NCAIR1/N-ATLaS`.
  3. Show the **Mode Toggle**: Toggle between Sovereign N-ATLAS Mode and Multi-Model Enterprise Grid.
* **Audio / Script:**
  > *"Behind the scenes, we engineered a resilient, dual-mode enterprise architecture. In our Admin Control Center, SabiRight features a dedicated Sovereign N-ATLAS management module.
  > 
  > Mode 1 is our Sovereign N-ATLAS priority mode, directly calling the `NCAIR1/N-ATLaS` model with intelligent fallback to prevent downtime during cold starts. 
  > 
  > With a single toggle, the platform can switch between Sovereign N-ATLAS mode and a multi-model enterprise grid, ensuring zero business interruption and full operational sovereignty."*

---

### Segment 5: Conclusion & National Impact (3:30 - 4:00)
* **Visual:** Presentation slide showing impact metrics: languages supported, statutory acts covered, and team details. Return to speaker on camera.
* **Audio / Script:**
  > *"SabiRight transforms N-ATLAS from an open-source model into national civic infrastructure. By giving every Nigerian access to their constitutional rights in the language of their heart, on the channels they already use, we are democratizing justice at scale.
  > 
  > We are proud to build with N-ATLAS for Nigeria. Thank you."*
